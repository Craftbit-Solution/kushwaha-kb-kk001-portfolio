Department profile

Fifteen years building the department's placement record, one batch at a time

Dr. Kulkarni joined the Department of BCA in 2011 and has served as Head of Department since 2019. Under her leadership the curriculum was rebuilt around three pillars: strong programming fundamentals, hands-on lab work, and a mandatory industry internship before final year.

She has guided 60+ final-year capstone projects, led two full syllabus revisions in line with university guidelines, and holds student consultation hours every week of the semester — no exceptions.

She received the Best HOD Award (2023, College Level), has published 12 papers in national and international journals, and has served as a resource person for 20+ faculty development programmes.
